# E-Commerce Sales & Customer Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

An interactive **E-Commerce Sales & Customer Analytics Dashboard** built using Microsoft Excel to analyze sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, product popularity, delivery performance, geographic trends, and customer satisfaction.

The dashboard converts raw e-commerce data into meaningful business insights using Excel formulas, calculated columns, Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, slicers, and interactive visualizations.

---

## Objectives

The main objective of this project is to create an interactive Excel dashboard that helps answer important business questions such as:

- How are sales and orders trending over time?
- Which products are most popular?
- How do customers prefer to place orders?
- What is the purchasing pattern of customers?
- Where are the customers located?
- How long does it take to deliver orders?
- How satisfied are customers?
- How do customer demographics affect purchasing behavior?

---

## Dataset

The dataset contains e-commerce customer and order information used for analyzing sales and customer behavior.

Key data attributes include:

The dashboard includes the following KPIs:

- **Total Orders**
- **Total Quantity**
- **Total Amount / Revenue**

These KPIs provide a quick overview of overall business performance.

## Dashboard Features

### 1. Sales Trend Analysis

A **13-week sales trend chart** is used to analyze changes in sales and order activity over time.

This helps identify:

- Sales growth and decline
- Weekly trends
- High-performing periods
- Changes in customer purchasing activity

---

### 2. Purchase Pattern Analysis

A grid chart is used to analyze how customers prefer to place their orders across different order modes and customer genders.

Order modes include:

- App
- Instagram
- Partner App
- Target.com
- Website

Customer segments include:

- Female
- Male
- Other
- Unknown

---

### 3. Quantity Distribution

A quantity distribution chart shows how many products customers typically purchase per order.

This helps understand:

- Common order quantities
- Purchasing patterns
- High-volume orders
- Customer buying behavior

---

### 4. Most Popular Products

A product popularity chart identifies products with the highest number of orders.

The analysis helps businesses understand:

- Best-selling products
- Product demand
- Customer preferences
- Products requiring higher inventory

---

### 5. Geographic Customer Analysis

A map visualization is used to analyze the geographic distribution of customers.

This helps identify:

- Customer concentration by location
- High-demand regions
- Geographic sales opportunities
- Regional customer trends

---

### 6. Delivery Performance

A delivery-time chart analyzes the number of orders based on **days required for delivery**.

This helps evaluate:

- Delivery speed
- Common delivery durations
- Delayed orders
- Overall shipping performance

---

### 7. Customer Satisfaction Analysis

A customer satisfaction chart analyzes customer ratings over time.

This helps understand:

- Customer satisfaction levels
- Rating distribution
- Monthly satisfaction trends
- Potential service improvement areas

---

### 8. Interactive Slicers

Interactive slicers allow users to filter the dashboard dynamically.

Available filters include:

**Order Mode**
- App
- Instagram
- Partner App
- Target.com
- Website

**Gender**
- Female
- Male
- Other
- Unknown

The charts and KPIs update based on the selected filters.
