# 📊 Customer Behavior Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes customer shopping behavior to identify patterns in purchasing, sales, revenue, customer demographics, subscription status, and product categories.

The project demonstrates practical Data Analytics skills using SQL and Power BI to transform customer data into meaningful business insights.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Understand customer purchasing behavior
- Analyze revenue and sales across product categories
- Compare customer behavior across different age groups
- Analyze subscription status
- Understand customer review ratings
- Identify important customer segments
- Generate actionable business insights

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- SQL
- Power BI
- Excel
- Data Analysis
- DAX

---

## 📊 Power BI Dashboard

The interactive Power BI dashboard provides insights into:

- Number of Customers
- Average Purchase Amount
- Average Review Rating
- Subscription Status
- Revenue by Category
- Sales by Category
- Revenue by Age Group
- Sales by Age Group
- Customer Demographics
- Shipping Type

---

## 📈 Key Insights

- Clothing generates the highest revenue among the analyzed categories.
- Clothing also has the highest sales volume.
- 27% of customers have an active subscription.
- Young Adults contribute the highest sales among the analyzed age groups.
- The average purchase amount is approximately $59.76.
- The average customer review rating is approximately 3.75.
